Choose a master phrase

One phrase opens the vault. Cerberus cannot reset it. Long word phrases are easier to type and harder to guess.

Save your recovery key

If you forget the master phrase, this key is the only way in. Cerberus shows it once and keeps no copy you can read.

1CRK12DEMO3AAAA4BBBB5CCCC 6DDDD7EEEE8FFFF9GGGG10HHHH 11JJJJ12KKKK13MMMM14NNNN15PPPP
Lost phrase and lost key means lost data. Nobody, including the Cerberus team, can open the vault without one of them.
